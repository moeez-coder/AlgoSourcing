# Vertical portfolio: every vertical in one standard format

Drafted 2026-09-29 by the master session from `client-base-vertical-analysis.md` (65 current/past clients) and
Algo's offer (Core: $20k/quarter for 40 showed meetings, ~$500 a meeting; 3x ROI needs ~$15k+ first-year value
per won client). V1-V4 are live (V4 since 2026-09-21); V1b and V5-V9 have groundwork done (2026-09-29, user-approved) and are waiting for their own sessions until the user approves them. When a proposal is approved,
it gets its own `verticals/vertical-N-<slug>.md`, a listbuild config, a data folder with a contacted ledger, and
HeyReach campaigns.

Shared rules for every vertical unless a card says otherwise (`icp-overview.md`): HQ in the US, UK or Europe with
founders locally present; revenue >= $1M; headcount >= 10; Nonprofit / Government / Educational excluded; people
Director and above (title guard in listbuild); dedup on LinkedIn URL; clients in `data/dnc_clients.csv` never
contacted; push only to Con Req and Open Check.

## V1: Staffing & Recruitment
- **Status / priority:** Live, P1.
- **Who:** agencies and firms that place permanent, contract or temporary staff, and executive search firms.
- **Why:**
  - **Client proof:** 35 of 65 clients (54%), plus case studies on the site.
  - **Offer fit:** a single placement fee ($15-30k) covers most of a quarter's fee.
- **Company filters:**
  - **Core LinkedIn industries:** Staffing and Recruiting, Executive Search Services, Temporary Help Services.
  - **Keyword-gated industries:** Human Resources Services, Human Resources. Keywords: staffing agency,
    recruitment agency, recruiting firm, executive search, headhunting, RPO, temporary / contract staffing,
    permanent placement.
- **People:** Founder, Owner, CEO, Managing Director, President, Partner, VP / Head / Director of Sales,
  Business Development, Client Services, Growth.
- **Segments (messaging and case studies):**

  | Segment | Proof clients |
  |---|---|
  | 1a. Healthcare & life-sciences staffing | Epic Physician, Epic Special Ed, Rosman, Meet Life Sciences |
  | 1b. Retained / boutique executive search | TTSP, Harrison Stone, Leah Yosef, SIG, Sequel |
  | 1c. Technical, engineering & industrial | Mastrovito, JBL, Core Group, Excel Resourcing, Astro Talent, Trillium |
  | 1d. IT, tech and finance/accounting | Brilliant, Abacus, Talent Edge, PTY Tech, Local World, Quanta |
  | 1e. Nearshore talent | Zipdev, Alcor |
- **Messaging angle:** guaranteed meetings with hiring managers and HR leaders instead of job-board spend or BD
  hires. Proof: Core Group Resources' first placement from cold outreach; Mastrovito's contract signing.
- **TAM (2026-09-29 run):**
  - Companies: ~33,735 core, plus ~2,412 keyword-gated.
  - People: ~116,574 director-plus. The main list has 59,926 people at 14,133 companies; there are 12,051
    candidates and 1,440 unverified.
- **HeyReach:** Con Req 567452 (PAUSED, ~23.9k already mid-flight); Open Check 567476 (FINISHED; pushing resumes it).
- **Next step:** user approval to push the 59,926 main list; decide whether to review the 16.7k held-back
  HR-services people (it includes some real staffing firms).

- Consumer / e-commerce, local trades, SaaS under ~$5k ACV (can't absorb ~$500 a meeting), and government- or
  procurement-led sales.
- Industrial manufacturing as a vertical (one client, Garrison Flood Control).
